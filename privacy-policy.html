Introduction

Welcome to ToolTari ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and tools (https://tooltari.in).

ToolTari provides free online PDF and image processing tools that work entirely in your browser. Unlike most online tools, we do not upload your files to any server. All processing happens locally on your device.

Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your data. By using our website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

IMPORTANT ToolTari does NOT collect, upload, or store your files.

We collect minimal information to improve our service:

  • Browser Information: Browser type, version, and language settings (automatically provided by your browser).
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and screen resolution.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, and features used (anonymous only).
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device (see Cookie Policy below).

What We DO NOT Collect:

❌ Your uploaded files
❌ Processed documents or images
❌ Personal identification information (no login/registration)
❌ File content or metadata
❌ Payment information (we are completely free)

How We Use Information

We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Improve Our Tools: Understand which tools are most popular to prioritize development.
  • Optimize Performance: Ensure our website loads quickly on all devices.
  • Analyze Trends: Track anonymous usage patterns to make data-driven decisions.
  • Display Relevant Ads: Google AdSense may use data to show personalized ads (see Third-Party Services).
  • Enhance Security: Protect against malicious activity and abuse.

We DO NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help us provide a better user experience. ToolTari uses cookies for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (Google Analytics).
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense may use cookies to display relevant advertisements.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website.

Managing Cookies: Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Instructions are typically found in your browser's "Help" or "Settings" menu.

Data Security & Privacy (Core)

🔒 Your Files Never Leave Your Device

This is our most important privacy promise:

✅ All tools run entirely in your web browser
✅ Files are NOT uploaded to any server
✅ Files are NOT stored, cached, or backed up
✅ No one at ToolTari can access your documents
✅ Processing happens locally using JavaScript libraries
✅ Once you close the page, all data is permanently gone

Unlike other "free" online tools that upload your sensitive documents to unknown servers, ToolTari is built different. We use modern browser technologies (PDF.js, pdf-lib, Canvas API) to process everything on your device.

This means:

  • Your confidential documents remain confidential
  • No data breaches from our servers (because we have no servers storing files)
  • No third-party access to your content
  • Complete legal compliance for sensitive industries (healthcare, legal, finance)

Security Best Practices: Always use our tools on trusted devices and keep your browser updated for the latest security patches.

Your Rights

As a ToolTari user, you have the following rights:

  • No Account Required: You can use all tools without creating an account or providing personal information.
  • Right to Access: Ask what data we have about you (very limited).
  • Right to Delete: Since we don't store files, no deletion is needed.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can disable cookies through your browser settings.
  • Right to Information: You can contact us with privacy questions anytime.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through our Contact Page.
