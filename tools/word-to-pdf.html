Understanding Word to PDF Conversion

DOCX (Microsoft Word Open XML Document) and PDF (Portable Document Format) serve different purposes. Word is designed for editing, while PDF is designed for sharing and printing. When converting between these formats, text content is preserved, but complex formatting like tables, columns, and images may not transfer perfectly.

Our converter uses JSZip to extract content from your DOCX file and jsPDF to generate a professional PDF document. The converter:

  • Extracts text content from document.xml inside the DOCX archive
  • Preserves paragraph structure and line breaks
  • Creates a clean, readable PDF with proper font handling
  • Adds page breaks for long documents
