Large images slow down websites, take up storage space, and are difficult to share. This guide shows you how to reduce image size online for free while maintaining quality.

Did you know? A 5MB image can be compressed to under 500KB with minimal quality loss.

Why Reduce Image Size?

  • Faster Websites: Smaller images load quicker
  • Email Attachments: Many email providers limit attachment size
  • Storage Savings: Save space on your device and cloud
  • Better SEO: Google favors faster-loading pages

Best Practices for Image Compression

  • Use JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency
  • Resize images before compressing for better results
  • Remove unnecessary metadata (EXIF data)
  • Consider WebP format for modern browsers
